# PRODUCT — 1-800 Contacts (descriptive, current-state)

- **Register:** brand (marketing/commerce landing).
- **Product purpose:** Sell contact lenses (and glasses, online vision exam, vision-
  insurance benefit lookup) direct to consumer with a best-price guarantee, free fast
  shipping, and prescription verification. "We Deliver. You Save.™"
- **Users:** Contact-lens wearers reordering or switching; first-time buyers chasing the
  new-customer discount; people renewing a prescription online.
- **Brand personality:** Playful, confident, irreverent, reassuring. Signals: "Huzzah for
  happy customers!", "FAQs: Freakin' Awesome Questions", "Gajillion Percent Promise",
  hand-drawn marker doodles ("Easy peasy", "Takes 10 minutes").
- **Design principles (observed):** bold navy + orange contrast; big friendly rounded type;
  pill CTAs; light-blue section rhythm; social proof + trust (stars, promise, payment badges).
